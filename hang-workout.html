How to use Abrahangs

Aim: Get stronger by following this sub-max hangs program.

  • Load your fingers to between 20% and 40% of bodyweight (bend your knees with your feet on the ground, till fingers are evenly loaded)
  • Do the full set once in the morning, and once in the evening, daily
  • You should notice some good gains in ~3 weeks if you're consistent
  • Shortcut method is to do it at the beginning of every climbing session
